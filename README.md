📂 Raw Data Overview

The dashboard is powered by a structured transactional dataset tracking unit pricing, sales volume, and realized revenue across sales representatives and regional branches.

<img width="482" height="546" alt="Raw Data" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fa6c58b2-7e7a-4f98-baef-bca69ccd896e" />


Data Fields:

Product: Item sold (e.g., Laptop, Monitor, Keyboard, Headset, Mouse)

Region: Sales territory (East, North, South, West)

Employee: Sales representative handling the account (e.g., Raj, Simran, Mohit, Anjali, Riya)

Month: Transaction period (January – June)

Quantity: Number of units sold

Price: Unit list price

Sales: Total gross value ($Quantity \times Price$)

Revenue: Net revenue generated

🔍 Core Visualizations & Features

Interactive Slicers:

Month Filter: Quickly filter through H1 data (Jan – Jun).

Region Filter: Segment data across East, North, South, and West territories.

Revenue by Month (Trend Line):

Highlights overall growth and seasonal spikes between January and June.

Revenue by Region (Bar Chart):

Visualizes territory contributions, spotlighting the South Region as the leading contributor (~₹1.92 Cr).

Top 3 Products by Revenue (Horizontal Bar Chart):

Quick visual comparison ranking high-value items: Laptops, Monitors, and Keyboards.
